RadarDesk

RadarDesk is an iOS app that reads public US regulatory filings — SEC Form 4 insider transactions, FINRA short volume, FINRA off-exchange volume — and combines them into one score per ticker, with the confidence and the data lag stated on screen rather than hidden.
