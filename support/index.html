What RadarDesk does

RadarDesk reads three public sources — SEC Form 4 insider filings, FINRA daily short volume, and FINRA off-exchange (ATS) weekly volume — and combines them into a single score per ticker, with the direction and the confidence stated separately from the number.

Common questions

A panel says “absent” rather than showing a number.

That is deliberate and it is not a fault. When a data source has published nothing for a symbol, RadarDesk shows the panel as absent instead of filling it with zero, because zero is a claim about the market and absence is not. The composite score is computed from the panels that are present and the completeness figure on screen tells you how much of the picture you are seeing.

Off-exchange volume looks weeks old.

It is. FINRA publishes ATS and non-ATS off-exchange volume on a two-to-four-week delay. That lag is a property of the data, not of the app, and RadarDesk labels it everywhere it appears rather than presenting stale figures as current.

The banner says data is degraded or stale.

One of the upstream publishers is late or unreachable. The app keeps showing the last data it has, with its age, rather than blanking the screen. It clears itself when the source returns.

Notifications are not arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for RadarDesk in iOS Settings, that quiet hours in Settings ▸ Alerts are not covering the current time, and that the alert rule is enabled. Alerts are always visible on the Alerts screen even when delivery is off, so if a rule fired you will see it there.

I reinstalled and my watchlist is gone.

Expected. RadarDesk has no account by design, so there is nothing that survives a reinstall to restore from. That is the direct cost of not identifying you; it is described in the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem with the data

If a figure looks wrong, email the ticker, the panel, what the app showed and what you expected, with the date. Filings get restated and amended and RadarDesk can be wrong too — a specific report is worth far more than a general one.
